# Enchanted Visuals

What's new:

Home – facilities: each of the six cards flips over in 3D when you click it. The back is gold and has more detail and opening hours.

Home – "The Royal Table" and "Sunset Rooftop": these photos tilt toward your mouse. Clicking one opens a pop-up like a book: the photo comes in on one side, the text page swings open, and its lines rise in one at a time.

Home – room "View Details": the pop-up now swings open in 3D instead of just fading in.

About – Mission, Vision and "Why choose us": these cards flip in 3D when clicked.

About – photos: the photos tilt on hover. Clicking one opens a full-screen viewer where the photo flies out of the spot you clicked. Moving to the next photo swings it like a carousel, with a row of small angled photos along the bottom to pick from.

Gallery: tiles turn in when they appear and tilt on hover. Clicking a tile opens the same 3D viewer. You can use the arrows, the arrow keys, swipe, or the small photos at the bottom.

Tested on a computer screen:

The facility cards flip.

The dining pop-up opens and closes with Escape, and the page scrolls normally again afterwards.

The About photo viewer opens and its "next" button works.

The Gallery viewer opens and the arrow key moves to the next photo.

Not finished:

One Gallery test didn't complete: clicking a small photo at the bottom of the viewer. I expect it works: the photo I tried was probably too far along to appear in the bottom row, rather than a fault.

Not checked yet: the Gallery category filters with the new tiles, the phone layout of these pop-ups, and whether the pages still avoid sideways scrolling on phones.
